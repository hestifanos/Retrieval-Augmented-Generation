The ARPANET delivered its first message in 1969 between UCLA and the Stanford Research Institute. The TCP/IP protocol suite was standardized in 1983 and remains the foundation of the modern internet. Tim Berners-Lee proposed the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN. The first graphical web browser to gain wide adoption was Mosaic, released in 1993. Domain names are resolved to IP addresses by the Domain Name System, a hierarchical and distributed database. HTTPS encrypts web traffic using TLS, protecting it from eavesdropping and tampering.
