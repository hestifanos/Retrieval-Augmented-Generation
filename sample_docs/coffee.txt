Coffee cultivation began in Ethiopia, where the Coffea arabica plant grows natively. Arabica beans account for roughly 60 percent of world production and are prized for a smoother, more complex flavor. Robusta beans contain nearly twice the caffeine of arabica and are hardier to grow. The ideal water temperature for brewing coffee is between 90 and 96 degrees Celsius. Espresso is produced by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee at around nine bars of pressure. Cold brew is made by steeping coarse grounds in cold water for twelve to twenty four hours, yielding lower acidity.
